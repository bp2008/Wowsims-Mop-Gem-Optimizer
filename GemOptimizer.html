The optimizer preserves exactly the socket bonuses the WowSims plan keeps: if WowSims color-matched every socket on an item, the gems that end up there (kept or replacement) are guaranteed to match too — so bonus stats are identical on both sides and cancel out of the comparison. If WowSims itself broke an item's bonus, every socket on that item is unconstrained. Imported gear knows its items and bonuses; hand-entered sockets are each treated as their own item with a bonus assumed worth keeping (set color Any to opt out). Prismatic and sha-touched sockets count as Any; meta sockets and engineering cogwheels are skipped. Gem stats sourced from the WowSims MoP database.
